# Product Strategy

## Product thesis

Argent should feel like a discreet, human-led matchmaking service supported by excellent technology—not an automated dating app with luxury styling.

The product creates value by helping Argent:

1. attract and qualify strong candidates;
2. build a trusted, permissioned candidate network;
3. operate high-touch matchmaking consistently;
4. run selective partner campaigns without surrendering platform control;
5. learn from introductions and relationship outcomes while preserving human judgment.

## Beachhead and pilot hypothesis

The first proving ground is a human-led local service pilot supported by one controlled Santa Barbara County recruiting campaign.

Proposed hypothesis:

> Argent can recruit and qualify a private local candidate pool, then help one matchmaker produce credible, mutually approved introductions for a small number of paying clients with materially less operational effort.

The first paying customer, package, price, duration, fulfillment promise, staffing envelope, and non-guarantees must be approved in Phase 0. Candidate acquisition is necessary but is not by itself proof that clients will pay or receive value.

A campaign may use a recruiting message such as:

> Montecito Matchmaker is seeking up to 100 bachelors and 100 bachelorettes in Santa Barbara County.

Final campaign language, quotas, eligibility, inclusive relationship/gender language, and partner relationship require approval before launch. “100/100” remains an unapproved recruiting target or capacity cap—not a success metric or guarantee.

## Primary users

### Paying matchmaking client

Wants discreet, curated introductions and confidence that Argent understands their goals, privacy needs, and standards.

### Candidate

May apply or be invited. Wants respectful treatment, clarity about status, control over information, and worthwhile introductions without public exposure.

### Argent matchmaker

Needs a reliable operating workspace for screening, interviewing, searching, shortlisting, introductions, feedback, follow-up, and relationship outcomes.

### Argent administrator

Manages staff access, campaigns, policies, configuration, audit review, support, and operational health.

### Campaign partner

Needs limited campaign visibility, attribution, and reporting without broad access to Argent's candidate network or private matchmaker notes.

## Product principles

- **Selective, not demeaning:** communicate standards and status clearly without ranking a person's worth.
- **Discreet by default:** reveal the minimum information needed at each step.
- **Human judgment is final:** AI may assist but does not silently accept, reject, rank, or introduce people.
- **Consent follows the data:** campaign participation, Argent network membership, sharing, and marketing permissions are separate.
- **Trust is part of luxury:** security, verification, responsive service, and accurate language matter more than decorative exclusivity.
- **Operations before spectacle:** optimize the matchmaking workflow before adding broad social or marketplace features.
- **Evidence over intuition:** product decisions should be tested with applicants, candidates, clients, and matchmakers.

## Jobs to be done

- Apply to be considered without exposing a public dating profile.
- Understand what Argent needs, what happens next, and how information will be used.
- Review applicants consistently and move them through a visible lifecycle.
- Find plausible candidates using hard constraints, soft preferences, context, and matchmaker judgment.
- Obtain permission from both people before an introduction.
- Learn from feedback without turning subjective outcomes into opaque automated scores.
- Launch and measure a controlled partner campaign without creating a separate tenant.

## Experience and brand direction

The selected visual direction is **Nocturne**: a discreet cinematic system using near-black aubergine, smoked plum, parchment, muted silver, and sparing oxblood. It combines condensed display typography with readable humanist interface typography, asymmetric grids, hairline structure, controlled imagery, and an operational density mode for staff work. The same semantic token system governs public web, staff web, iOS, and Android while allowing native platform behavior.

Nocturne is selected but remains pending final `ARG-004` approval. The canonical visual and token contract is [design-system.md](design-system.md).

Avoid generic gold-on-black “VIP” motifs, cream-and-forest luxury templates, centered editorial-serif hero defaults, decorative card grids, status theatrics, gamification, public popularity metrics, and swipe-based interaction.

“Elite” should be demonstrated through discretion, service quality, curation, and confidence—not exclusionary copy.

## Business-model questions requiring validation

- Who pays: clients, candidates, partners, or a combination?
- Is an interview or verification fee appropriate, and what does it guarantee?
- What services may be premium without corrupting match quality?
- How are partner referrals and campaign attribution compensated?
- What happens when a client or candidate withdraws?
- Which services are refundable, and how are disputes handled?

Paid placement in match rankings is a current non-goal because it could undermine trust.

Argent is initially a concierge service platform, not a marketplace. Marketplace listings, seller tooling, automated payouts, and open partner self-service require a separate business decision.

## Service promises and prohibited claims

Before accepting payment or real applications, approved copy must define what Argent provides and must not imply:

- guaranteed admission;
- guaranteed verification or safety;
- guaranteed introduction, date, match, or relationship;
- that an invite code is an endorsement;
- that payment improves compatibility ranking;
- that AI output is objective truth.

## Success measures

Private-beta targets must be approved before implementation. Candidate measures:

- qualified applications per campaign;
- application completion and withdrawal rates;
- median time from submission to decision;
- eligible candidates per active client;
- time to first viable shortlist and introduction;
- mutual introduction acceptance rate;
- completed-date and repeat-date rate;
- candidate and client satisfaction;
- human override rate for AI recommendations;
- privacy, safety, and support incidents;
- campaign conversion and source attribution;
- operational time per reviewed applicant and completed introduction.

The authoritative metric contracts, targets, owners, and stop/iterate/expand rules belong in [metrics.md](metrics.md).
