# ARG-003 Low-Fidelity Concept Stimuli

These are copy-level stimuli for research only. They are not approved public
copy, campaign copy, legal notices, consent text, or UI implementation.

## Concept A — Public Argent application

### Version A1

> Argent Matchmaking is a private, human-led matchmaking service for people who
> value discretion, clarity, and curated introductions. Anyone may apply.
> Admission to the Argent network is reviewed by our team and is not guaranteed.

### Version A2

> Apply to be considered for Argent's private matchmaking network. Your
> application is reviewed by humans. If there may be a fit, we may ask for more
> information or invite you to the next step.

### Research prompts

- Does “anyone may apply” feel believable and respectful?
- Does “not guaranteed” feel clear or cold?
- Does “private network” imply exclusivity, safety, status, or something else?
- What response timeline would you expect?

## Concept B — Montecito/Santa Barbara campaign

### Version B1

> Argent is opening a limited Santa Barbara County/Montecito beta campaign to
> build a private candidate network for a small number of human-led
> introductions. This campaign is our first local test ground; Argent is not
> limited to Santa Barbara County.

### Version B2

> Montecito Matchmaker is partnering with Argent to invite applications from up
> to 100 men and 100 women in the Santa Barbara County area. Applying does not
> guarantee admission, verification, or an introduction.

### Research prompts

- Does this sound like Argent is only local?
- Does “up to 100 men and 100 women” sound like a quota, promise, or capacity
  cap?
- Does partner wording make control and privacy clear?
- Does the language include people respectfully?

## Concept C — Intake mode choice

### Version C1

> Choose how you want to complete your application. You can answer structured
> questions, use a guided conversational path, or combine both. Each path
> creates the same reviewable application. You approve, edit, or reject any
> AI-assisted summary before it is used.

### Version C2

> Prefer to talk it through? A conversational path can help you draft fuller
> answers. It is optional. You stay in control of what is saved, what remains
> private, and what is submitted for review.

### Research prompts

- Which path would you choose?
- Does conversational intake feel helpful, intrusive, premium, or risky?
- Do you understand that AI does not evaluate you?
- What privacy concern appears first?

## Concept D — Status language

### Status set D1

| Internal state | User-facing candidate copy |
| --- | --- |
| Submitted | Your application has been received. |
| In review | Argent is reviewing your application. |
| Needs information | We need a little more information before review can continue. |
| Waitlisted | We are not moving forward right now, but may revisit your application if the campaign or client needs change. |
| Not admitted | Argent is not able to move forward with your application at this time. |
| Withdrawn | Your application has been withdrawn. |

### Research prompts

- Which state feels most unclear?
- Does any state feel like a judgment of personal worth?
- What support or explanation would you expect?
- Is “not admitted” better or worse than “declined”?

## Concept E — Paying client service promise

### Version E1

> Paying clients retain Argent for a defined, human-led search process:
> criteria work, discreet candidate review, curated introduction coordination,
> and follow-up. Argent can promise the process it controls. Argent does not
> promise a match, date, relationship, or fixed number of suitable people.

### Version E2

> Argent's paid service is high-touch and human-led. Pricing and packages are
> configured by Argent and processed through Stripe. Payment does not improve a
> person's compatibility ranking or guarantee an introduction.

### Research prompts

- What would you expect to pay for?
- Does the no-guarantee boundary feel fair?
- Does Stripe/payment wording create trust or distraction?
- What package details are missing before payment?

## Concept F — AI assistance boundary

### Version F1

> Argent may use AI to help summarize approved application answers, identify
> missing information, and help matchmakers retrieve relevant candidate facts.
> AI does not admit, reject, rank, verify, or introduce people.

### Version F2

> AI assistance is source-grounded and human-reviewed. If AI proposes a profile
> summary from your answers, you can approve, edit, reject, or keep proposed
> fields private before submission.

### Research prompts

- What do you think AI is doing?
- What do you think AI is not allowed to do?
- Which sentence increases trust?
- Which sentence creates concern?
