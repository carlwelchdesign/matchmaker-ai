# ARG-003 Moderator Guide

## Purpose

Run concept and language testing for applicant, candidate, client, campaign,
status, and AI-assistance language before user-facing implementation.

Use with:

- [ARG-003 protocol](../ARG-003-concept-language-testing.md)
- [Concept stimuli](ARG-003-concept-stimuli.md)
- [Consent and session script](../consent-and-session-script.md)
- [Research session notes template](../../templates/research-session-notes.md)

## Standard run of show

| Segment | Time | Activity |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Opening and consent | 5 min | Read purpose, non-goals, note/quote/recording consent |
| Warm-up | 5 min | Ask participant how they would describe a premium private service |
| Concept review | 30 min | Review 2-4 concept cards, depending on segment |
| Language sort | 15 min | Classify words as clear, confusing, concerning, or premium |
| Closeout | 5 min | Ask what Argent should say more clearly or avoid saying |

## Participant routing

| Segment | Required concepts |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant/candidate | A, B, C, D |
| Potential paying client | A, D, E |
| Founder/operator | A, B, C, D, E |
| Accessibility-oriented reviewer | A, C, D, F |

Concepts are defined in [ARG-003 concept stimuli](ARG-003-concept-stimuli.md).

## Warm-up prompts

1. When you hear “private matchmaking,” what do you expect?
2. What would make the service feel high quality?
3. What would make it feel uncomfortable or unserious?
4. What would you need to understand before sharing information?

## Concept review prompts

For each concept:

1. In your own words, what is Argent offering?
2. What do you believe happens next?
3. Who do you think this is for?
4. Who do you think gets to see your information?
5. Does anything sound guaranteed?
6. Does anything sound exclusionary or judgmental?
7. Does anything imply payment changes admission or match priority?
8. Does anything imply AI is judging compatibility?
9. What feels premium?
10. What needs to be clearer?

## Language sort

Ask the participant to classify each term:

- clear and appropriate;
- premium but needs explanation;
- confusing;
- concerning;
- should not be used.

Terms:

- elite;
- selective;
- private network;
- accepted;
- waitlisted;
- verified;
- invite;
- referral;
- match;
- curated introduction;
- AI-assisted;
- human-reviewed;
- confidential.

## Segment-specific probes

### Applicant/candidate

- Would you apply after reading this?
- What would stop you?
- What would you expect to hear after applying?
- Would structured, conversational, or hybrid intake feel most appropriate?
- What would make voice input unacceptable?

### Potential paying client

- What would you expect to pay for?
- What information would you need before paying?
- What would make the service feel worth a premium price?
- What outcome would you understand is not guaranteed?
- What support or human contact would you expect?

### Accessibility-oriented reviewer

- Is the reading burden reasonable?
- Which terms need plain-language definitions?
- Would the path work for keyboard, screen reader, low vision, motor, language,
  cognitive, or privacy-constrained users?
- Does conversational/voice intake feel like an optional accommodation or a
  pressure point?

## Capture rules

- Capture interpretations, not personal dating history.
- Mark emotional reactions separately from comprehension.
- Do not record sensitive demographic or identity details unless specifically
  approved for an accessibility/fairness purpose.
- Tag downstream impact immediately:
  - `ARG-004` brand approval
  - `ARG-005` lifecycle language
  - `ARG-006` privacy/legal review
  - `ARG-016` trust claims
  - `ARG-018` application/consent prototype
  - `ARG-026` matching claims
  - `ARG-027` conversational intake

## Closeout questions

1. What would you tell a friend Argent is?
2. What should Argent never imply?
3. What term should we replace?
4. What is the clearest sentence you saw?
5. What is the riskiest sentence you saw?
