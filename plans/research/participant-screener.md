# ARG-002/ARG-003/ARG-617 Participant Screener

## Purpose

Recruit appropriate research participants while avoiding unnecessary sensitive
screening data.

## Do not collect

- exact net worth, income, account balances, or financial documents;
- dating history details;
- intimate relationship details;
- protected-class information unless counsel approves a specific accessibility
  or fairness research purpose;
- identity documents;
- phone/email/social handles in repository planning files.

## Segment targets

| Segment | Minimum | Relevant ticket | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Founder/operator | 1 | ARG-002 ARG-003 | Confirm actual operating model and copy intent |
| Practicing matchmaker or high-touch operator | 2 | ARG-002 ARG-617 | Validate workflow, review, shortlist, introduction, and analytics-reconciliation patterns |
| Applicant/candidate representative | 3 | ARG-003 | Test application, selectivity, status, privacy, and intake language |

## Screener questions

Use these as discussion prompts, not scored qualification rules.

### Founder/operator

1. Are you responsible for defining Argent's service experience or operating
   policies?
2. Can you walk through representative cases without revealing identifying
   private details?
3. Which decisions are you comfortable treating as project-owner direction?

### Matchmaker/high-touch operator

1. Have you personally managed client intake, candidate evaluation, or curated
   introductions in a high-trust service?
2. Can you describe workflow patterns without revealing client/candidate
   identities?
3. Are you comfortable discussing what should remain human-led?
4. Are you comfortable identifying where software often gets in the way?

### Applicant/candidate representative

1. Would you consider applying to a selective private matchmaking network or
   similar high-trust personal service?
2. Are you comfortable reviewing low-fidelity copy and explaining what it means
   to you?
3. Are you willing to avoid sharing intimate personal details during the
   session?
4. Do you use assistive technology or alternate input modes that you want the
   team to consider? Answering is optional.

### Potential paying client

1. Would you consider paying for a high-touch, human-led personal service such
   as matchmaking, recruiting, coaching, concierge travel, or advisory support?
2. What information would you need before paying?
3. Are you comfortable reacting to service promise and status language without
   discussing your personal dating history?

### Accessibility-oriented reviewer

1. Can you review clarity, reading burden, input-mode choice, and status
   comprehension?
2. Can you identify where copy or interaction patterns could disadvantage users
   with cognitive, sensory, motor, language, or privacy constraints?

## Exclusion criteria

Do not include a participant if:

- they expect professional legal, clinical, safety, or relationship advice from
  the session;
- they need to disclose sensitive personal material to participate;
- they cannot consent to research notes;
- their participation would create a conflict of interest that cannot be
  disclosed and managed.

## Recruiting notes

- Prefer people who can reason about the service without needing to disclose
  private romantic history.
- Treat wealthy/high-status participants as a service-fit segment, not as a
  proxy for relationship quality.
- Do not over-index on Santa Barbara residents; Santa Barbara/Montecito is the
  first beta campaign, not the full ICP.
