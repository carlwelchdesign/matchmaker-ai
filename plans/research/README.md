# Research Gate Artifacts

This folder contains executable research protocols, evidence templates, and
synthesis criteria for Phase 0 decision gates.

## Operating files

- [research-operations-runbook.md](research-operations-runbook.md) — session
  execution, storage, and synthesis process.
- [participant-screener.md](participant-screener.md) — low-risk screener for
  required participant segments.
- [consent-and-session-script.md](consent-and-session-script.md) — plain
  language readout and consent confirmation.
- [research-synthesis-tracker.md](research-synthesis-tracker.md) — coverage,
  finding, and decision tracker.
- [session-materials/ARG-002-moderator-guide.md](session-materials/ARG-002-moderator-guide.md) —
  founder/matchmaker workflow moderator guide.
- [session-materials/ARG-003-moderator-guide.md](session-materials/ARG-003-moderator-guide.md) —
  concept/language moderator guide.
- [session-materials/ARG-003-concept-stimuli.md](session-materials/ARG-003-concept-stimuli.md) —
  low-fidelity copy stimuli for testing.
- [synthetic-dry-runs/ARG-002-synthetic-case-walkthroughs.md](synthetic-dry-runs/ARG-002-synthetic-case-walkthroughs.md) —
  synthetic workflow dry run, not participant evidence.
- [synthetic-dry-runs/ARG-003-synthetic-concept-review.md](synthetic-dry-runs/ARG-003-synthetic-concept-review.md) —
  synthetic language and concept dry run, not participant evidence.

Research artifacts must separate:

- confirmed founder or owner direction;
- participant evidence from interviews, usability tests, or concept reviews;
- Codex/planning inferences;
- unresolved assumptions.

Do not mark a research ticket `Done` until its evidence table links to real or
explicitly synthetic sessions, the synthesis is complete, and downstream
decisions have been updated.
