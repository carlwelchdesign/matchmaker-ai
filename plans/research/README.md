# Research Gate Artifacts

This folder contains executable research protocols, evidence templates, and
synthesis criteria for Phase 0 decision gates.

Research artifacts must separate:

- confirmed founder or owner direction;
- participant evidence from interviews, usability tests, or concept reviews;
- Codex/planning inferences;
- unresolved assumptions.

Do not mark a research ticket `Done` until its evidence table links to real or
explicitly synthetic sessions, the synthesis is complete, and downstream
decisions have been updated.
