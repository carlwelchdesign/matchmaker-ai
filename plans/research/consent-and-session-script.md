# Consent and Session Script

## Opening script

Use this before every `ARG-002` or `ARG-003` session.

> Thanks for helping with early research for Argent Matchmaking. We are testing
> how the service should work and how the language is understood. This is not an
> application, interview, matchmaking evaluation, verification step, or offer of
> service. Nothing you say here will affect admission, matching, or service
> priority.

> Please avoid sharing names, contact information, intimate relationship
> details, health information, identity documents, exact finances, or anything
> you would not want included in redacted planning notes.

> I may take notes. With your permission, I may use short anonymized quotes in
> internal planning. Recording or transcription is separate and optional. You can
> decline to answer any question or stop the session at any time.

## Consent confirmation

Record one:

- Notes only: yes/no.
- Short anonymized quotes: yes/no.
- Recording: yes/no.
- Transcript: yes/no.
- Follow-up contact: yes/no.

If recording or transcript consent is not explicitly granted, do not record or
transcribe.

## Research non-goals to restate

- We are not evaluating the participant.
- We are not collecting real application data.
- We are not promising acceptance, introductions, outcomes, safety, or
  verification.
- We are not testing whether AI can judge compatibility.
- We are not asking for private romantic history.

## Closeout script

> Thank you. We will use this feedback to improve the workflow, copy, and
> research plan. If any note seems too identifying or sensitive, tell us and we
> will remove or redact it. The next step is synthesis, not product launch.

## Researcher reminders

- Redirect oversharing.
- Ask for interpretation, not intimate details.
- Label assumptions immediately.
- Do not turn a participant reaction into an approved decision without synthesis
  and owner review.
