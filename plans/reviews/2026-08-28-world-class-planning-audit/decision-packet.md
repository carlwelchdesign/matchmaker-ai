# World-Class Planning Audit Decision Packet

## Executive conclusion

Argent's plan is directionally excellent: its human-led service thesis,
candidate protections, consent and provenance boundaries, synthetic-evidence
discipline, modular architecture, and refusal to automate consequential judgment
are stronger than most early-stage product plans.

It is not yet world-class as an executable delivery system. The largest risk is
not missing features; it is incoherence between approved sequencing, live work,
artifact maturity, ownership, ticket size, and machine-enforced readiness. The
portfolio has also invested more deeply in synthetic candidate intelligence than
the evidence currently supports for buyer demand, service capacity, commercial
economics, and the paying-client journey.

The audit recommends four new bounded outcomes, a clarified ARG-617 parent, and
targeted expansions to existing tickets. It does **not** recommend a broad
rewrite, new architecture program, marketplace, warehouse, AI epic, CMS, or
public DevRel program.

A staff-architecture cross-challenge then tested the consolidated proposal for
duplication and sequencing. It removed proposed standalone client/server and
event-governance tickets in favor of narrow ARG-101 and ARG-110 expansions, and
rejected duplicate ARG-617 children already owned elsewhere in the backlog.

## Immediate P0 decisions before further implementation

1. **Make workflow authority explicit.** Use Asana as the operational status
   authority and the repository as the durable planning/evidence authority.
   Separate delivery status from artifact maturity and reconcile the eight stale
   repository `In progress` rows.
2. **Enforce authorization to start.** Move ARG-024 and ARG-025 forward, harden
   the next-work planner, assign named owners/reviewers and critical-risk
   authorities, and protect `main` through a narrowed ARG-100.
3. **Return to evidence-first sequencing.** Run one target-buyer
   problem/commitment study and complete the service/capacity/commercial decision
   package before approving deeper product implementation.
4. **Fix two verified architecture regressions.** Remove browser imports of the
   server-designated domain package and add the admin deployable target to image
   scanning/SBOM parity.
5. **Right-size ARG-617.** Preserve its merged synthetic work, make the outcome
   a parent, link existing access, telemetry, persistence, and release owners,
   and add only a distinct representative-research child if needed.
6. **Complete the service and domain contracts before real data.** Approve the
   paying-client/member service blueprint, lifecycle ownership, research
   operations, field policy, permission decisions, identity merge/split, release
   bundles, and executable deletion behavior before persistence or outreach.

## P1 hardening before pilot or private beta

- Approve a privacy-safe measurement architecture with canonical KPI contracts,
  computation lineage, deletion/recompute behavior, differencing defense, and
  reconciliation.
- Add a reusable accessible component/state-pattern system rather than scaling
  one-off prototype UI.
- Reconcile AI ADR authority and turn evaluation, execution-envelope security,
  model lifecycle, and human review into reproducible release controls before
  any provider activation.
- Add versioned support matrices, dependency degradation behavior, detection and
  resilience evidence, audit durability, and a computed release manifest.
- Normalize commercial referral concepts only after deciding whether any
  compensated-referral model belongs in the pilot.

## P2 work to defer

- Subscription mechanics, marketplace/payment orchestration, generalized data
  infrastructure, public DevRel, generic CMS, native expansion, and decorative
  premium polish.
- These may become rational later, but none is evidence-backed as current
  critical-path work.

## Decisions requested from Carl Welch

### Decision 1 — Workflow authority

Approve or revise:

- Asana is authoritative for operational workflow status and the single-WIP
  constraint.
- The repository is authoritative for durable scope, dependencies, acceptance,
  and evidence.
- Conflicts block new work until an owner-approved reconciliation is recorded.

### Decision 2 — New-ticket set

Approve, reject, or combine the four proposed new outcomes in `ticket-deltas.md`.
The audit recommends keeping all four at P0 relative to their stated gates.

### Decision 3 — ARG-617 structure

Approve retaining ARG-617 as a parent/outcome, linking existing tickets for its
remaining implementation gates, and creating only a representative-matchmaker
research child if the outcome is not already owned. No merged evidence would be
discarded or relabeled as production proof.

### Decision 4 — Existing-ticket expansions

Approve the grouped acceptance and dependency changes in `ticket-deltas.md`,
especially the immediate re-sequencing of ARG-024, ARG-025, and ARG-100.

### Decision 5 — Next authorized work

Approve planning-control reconciliation and buyer/service/commercial evidence as
the next work. Do not resume synthetic product expansion merely because it can
be implemented without real data.

## Safety and scope boundary

or product behavior was changed by this audit. This packet does not authorize
participant recruitment, real candidate data, persistence, identity resolution,
analytics transport, provider activation, production access, billing, referral
compensation, or consequential automation.
