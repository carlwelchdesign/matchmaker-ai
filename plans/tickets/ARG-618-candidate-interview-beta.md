## Outcome

A small, explicitly consenting cohort establishes whether the adaptive interview is more useful and less burdensome while preserving control, accessibility, comparability, privacy, and cost limits.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Named owners approve research protocol, cohort, thresholds, incident path, rollback, retention, and participant communications.
- [ ] Structured and text-first modes run before voice; voice requires a separate gate and cohort.
- [ ] Candidate control, question quality, source support, trust, fairness/accessibility, operations, and economics metrics are reviewed together.
- [ ] Results distinguish mode effects, cohort limitations, missing data, and human-review effects.
- [ ] Prohibited-inference, oversharing, injection, deletion, mid-session flag disable, provider outage, and cost-cap tests pass.
- [ ] The team records a go, revise, or stop decision; no uniqueness or superiority marketing claim is made without comparative evidence.
